GravityControl changelog
========================

1.0   Release. Keyboard: Right Shift (tap = shift, hold = reset). On a different game build the mod
      warns once at start (VersionWarning) and runs anyway. Shorter ini comments and readme.
      Release. The mod is now called GravityControl: gravitycontrol.dll, gravitycontrol_config.ini,
      gravitycontrol.log. (Development builds up to this point were named GravityShift.)
1.18  Files renamed for consistency: gravityshift.dll, gravityshift_config.ini, gravityshift.log.
      Delete the old GravityShift.ini and GravityShift.log when upgrading.
1.17  The [Transition] settings are internal now (the game's own duration, eased blend and pivot).
1.16  No gameplay change. The MinHook third-party notices are embedded in the DLL and reproduced in
      the readme instead of shipping as a separate file.
1.15  Upward ray: a second sweep straight up; the closer of the forward and upward surfaces wins,
      so you can flip upside down onto a ceiling above you. [Ray] UpwardRay toggles it.
1.14  Fixed the ray never detecting anything (the engine's hit filter answers "ignore this hit",
      and 1.13 had it backwards). Fall respawns now reset gravity normally while the mod is engaged,
      and Reset works after a respawn left you on a turned plane.
1.13  Fixed a crash on the first shift (hit filter calling convention).
1.12  The ray starts from the player's centre; the player's own capsule is filtered out of the sweep.
      StartOffset removed.
1.11  Removed the ceiling probe diagnostics and the QueryFlags / ForwardSign / [HitRecord] settings
      (now internal constants).
1.10  Keys and pad buttons are ignored while a game menu is open (wardrobe, shop, talents, ability
      and melee select). [General] BlockInMenus.
1.9   Shifting requires the Gravity Anomaly ability to be unlocked in the save, the same check the
      game's own anomaly scan makes. [General] RequireAnomalyUnlocked.
1.8   [Blockers] option to switch off collision with a physics layer (experimental, off by default).
1.7   Ceiling probe (removed again in 1.11).
1.6   The new up is always a world axis (AxisOnly). Entering one of the game's anomaly zones hands
      the plane to the game; shifting from inside one clears its bookkeeping.
1.5   Controller support (d-pad up: tap = shift, hold = reset). Over-the-edge probe. Axis snapping.
1.4   Fixed crashes from a wrong pivot argument; the game's eased transition is used.
1.1   First working build: the camera turned, the character did not yet.
1.0   Initial build.
